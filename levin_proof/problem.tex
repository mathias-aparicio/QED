We consider the following n player game. Every player has a pile of infintely many hats on their heads. Each hat is either black or white. The colors of hats is sampled indepedently with probability 1/2 for each color. Each player can see the hats of every other player but not their own hats. The game is cooperative. The players simultaneously say a number. If everyone says the number of a hat on their head which is black, they win, otherwise they lose. The player may agree on a strategy before the hats are sampled but may not communicate once they have the hats on their heads. Let p_n be the supremum over strategies of the probability that they win . Prove of disprove that p_n goes to 0 when n goes to infinity.
